Gummy Joy

A calm match-3 game for people with intellectual disabilities. Nothing to lose, nothing to read, no ads.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to SUPPORT-EMAIL-TO-ADD. Replies usually within a few days.

For caregivers

Hold two fingers on the big candy at the top of the Play screen for three seconds to open the caregiver panel: sound, music and vibration, high contrast, number of candy colors, special candies, session length, levels finished this week, and start again from level 1.

To keep the player inside the game, turn on Guided Access in iOS Settings > Accessibility, then triple-click the side button while the game is open.

Purchases

The first 20 levels are free. "All levels" is a one-time purchase in the caregiver panel, shared with your Family Sharing group. Restore it from the same panel after a new install. Institutions can buy in volume through Apple Business Manager and Apple School Manager.
