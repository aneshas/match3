Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Gummy Joy is a game for people with intellectual disabilities, made by Anes Hasicic.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kits. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other personal data.

What stays on your device

The current level, a short log of finished levels (dates only, for the caregiver panel's "levels finished" counts) and the caregiver settings (sound, music, vibration, high contrast, color cap, session length) are stored on the device only, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

The one-time "All levels" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app only learns whether the purchase exists on your Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy covers the transaction: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Purchases sit in a caregiver panel behind a hidden gesture and a parental gate.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date. It will never add data collection without an app update that says so.
